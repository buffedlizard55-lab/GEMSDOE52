What GEMSDOE52 ships

A 40,532-pixel emission built as H32-D flank-{3*100} m prune + 3,000 A-only co-training discoveries + 200 B-only "suspect artefact" cells. The flank prune matches the structure of the GEMSDOE32 0.2778 file (0 pixels within 300 m of the catalogue), but the off-catalogue footprint is built on a different hypothesis family: a classifier-based co-training disagreement rule with empirical independence gating and per-cell geological reasoning. See the Hypotheses page for the physical signatures.
