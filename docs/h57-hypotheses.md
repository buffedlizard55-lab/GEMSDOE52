## Competition context and why this is a research test

The official public board was read on 2026-10-07: xiaofanhu 0.3774 (#1), DARD 0.3195 (#7), and extradr19 0.2778 (#13). The public table reports team-level best scores, not candidate filenames, raster hashes, or upload receipts. Local filename-to-score associations remain owner-reported; no H57 local metric can prove a board gain.

The repo's best publicly reported local artifact is described as H33 (`h33-h33-2-b2`, 0.2778), but that pairing is not organizer-authenticated. Byte-level local forensics record a related raster made by deleting near-trace, off-catalogue flank dots from its base; this is evidence that placement can matter, not proof of why the organizer score changed. H57 therefore tests a new physical signal against spatially blocked catalogue-holdout proxies and will make no numerical public-score forecast. A mapped catalogue zero is not a verified geological absence, and catalogue recovery is not the competition's hidden-new-fault evaluation.

DrivenData's problem statement says the known-fault inventory is incomplete / potentially inaccurate and describes expert review of new faults. That supports looking for unmapped structures; it does **not** establish that any one absent trace is buried, geothermal, or a fault. The official USGS/DOE Great Basin blind-system report supports integrated gravity, magnetic, structural, thermal and geochemical evidence as a way to investigate blind systems, not as validation of these predictions. The supplied `training_features.tif` band-6 tag is wrong: on 150,000 sampled overlapping pixels its Spearman correlation with GeoDAWN TC is 0.999950 (see [`evidence/h57_band6_identity.json`](../evidence/h57_band6_identity.json)); the corrected owner-mirror layer routing places TC in View B. No claim is based on the TIFF tag alone.

## Frozen H57-1 protocol

1. **Feature routing.** Use `src/gems55/radlayers.py`'s exact registered channel lists, `VIEW_A` and `VIEW_B`. Band 6/TC must not appear in A. B includes the competition TC grid, GeoDAWN Th/K and U/K steps/edges, DEM/scarp channels and LiDAR features plus a LiDAR coverage indicator. Stack encoding is the legacy `gems52.cotrain.rank_u8` helper's per-channel linear min–max map to uint8 0–255 (not an empirical CDF rank); NaNs map to zero, with LiDAR coverage explicit. This is label-free and uses no evaluation-label statistics. No previous prediction TIFF, prior mask, test label, or scored-file value enters model fitting, pseudo-label selection or emission.
2. **Core footprint / spatial folds.** Eligible pixels are the finite intersection of all 19 core bands. Apply `src/gems52/spatial.folds(catalogue, eligible, buffer_px=80)`: four fixed quadrants, whole original 8-connected catalogue components assigned by majority quadrant, held-component support region retained, and no train/evaluation overlap. The largest registered filter support is 36 px; the 80 px training buffer exceeds twice that. Each fold must show zero shared train/truth components and a measured ≥80 px train-to-region distance.
3. **Training targets.** Fit A and B separately using the same training positives and sampled negatives for each fold, HGB settings in the JSON registration, seed 520208 + fold, and a 300 m exclusion collar around catalogue positives for negative sampling. “Negative” denotes catalogue-zero proxy only. Model probabilities are not calibrated hidden-fault posteriors.
4. **OOF independence gate (must run before any exchange).** Measure both views' negative mean-squared error and false-positive rate in 50×50-pixel blocks on finite predictions for the held-out region, outside a 3-pixel collar around all catalogue positives. Use each view's training-unlabelled 99th-percentile score for the FPR threshold. A 50×50 block enters the diagnostic only with at least 32 finite proxy-negative predictions. Compute Pearson and tie-aware Spearman per fold and pooled. Require at least 20 non-degenerate blocks and absolute correlations <0.60 for **both** diagnostics in every fold and pooled. Undefined, insufficient or strongly correlated errors mean **disable co-training**; no positive-only or pixel-correlation fallback.
5. **Pseudo-labels.** Only if step 4 passes: one exchange round, positive pseudo-labels only, one view above its training-unlabelled 99th percentile while the receiver lies between its 40th and 80th training-unlabelled percentiles. Select whole 8-connected proposal components of at least 5 cells that remain wholly in that fold's training domain and a single 50×50 block, do not touch a forbidden labelled/collar pixel, and are capped at 2,000 pixels per donor per fold. Each pseudo-positive has sample weight 0.25. Zero evaluation pixels may enter pseudo-labels. In every outer fold, require at least one accepted whole segment in each direction (A→B and B→A) before fitting or scoring the round-1 primary; if either direction is empty in any fold, disable the exchange globally and use the B-only research fallback.
6. **Primary field, frozen.** Use round-1 probabilities. Define A-only/B-only by the confidence/abstention thresholds estimated from the round-0 supervised models on training-unlabelled pixels; reuse those exact cutoffs for the round-0 control and round-1 candidate, without post-exchange re-estimation. Concordant means both views exceed their confidence threshold. On the fold's prediction region, `field0 = 0.5*pA + 0.5*pB`; set A-only pixels to `pA*(0.35 + 0.40*depth_rank)`, B-only pixels to `0.25*pB`, concordant pixels to `min(pA,pB)`. `depth_rank` is the label-free rank of band 15 within the core eligible footprint. If the independence gate fails, this primary field is **not fit or scored as co-training**; the registered research-only fallback is B-only and must be named as such.
7. **Matched controls.** Same folds, positive/negative rows, learner, seed, placement and per-fold budget for A-only, corrected B-only, early fusion, max(A,B) union, round-0 no-exchange disagreement router, and the round-1 primary when allowed. The fold budget is `round(37,654 * evaluation_region_px / eligible_px)`. Place each field with `gems52.emit.greedy_emit`, projected DTI 0, pool `min(400000, max(16*fold_budget, 10000))` (matching the prior 80-pixel validation runner), and the exact official triangular 300 m metric. The emitter receives no truth. Select the strongest control once by its mean over all four folds, then use that same arm for all paired fold differences.
8. **Slot gate.** Candidate must exceed the best **same-run** control by at least +0.005 mean DTI, have positive paired differences in ≥3/4 folds, and exceed the previous best comparable 80-pixel mean (H55-1 paired-shoulder refit: 0.1970317574) by at least +0.005. Independence, leakage, format, canonical decoded-pattern distinctness, non-literal prior-union / same-run A/B-union checks and reasoning completeness must also pass. Report the all-prior support-novelty fraction, but do not use it as a promotion gate: dense historical diagnostic rasters can saturate the comparison union without making two decoded predictions identical. Any failure means **zero slots and not approved to submit**. Passing this catalogue proxy gate still would not authenticate a public score or establish hidden-fault generalization.
9. **Fallback and output.** If co-training is disabled/fails, emit only the preregistered corrected B-only baseline as a research artifact, not as a co-training result. Otherwise export the locked primary field's metric-aware emission even if it misses the slot gate, clearly marked research-only. In both cases use a new model inference, never copy/modify a prior prediction; write one single-band float32, finite `[0,1]` GeoTIFF on the sample grid, no negative nodata sentinel, zero outside footprint, then independently re-read and compare decoded pixels against every supplied aligned prior. A decoded-pattern pass and a format pass do not imply scientific quality or organizer acceptance.
