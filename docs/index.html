01 The one-sentence version

Most of the score still available in this competition is placement, not prediction: under the published metric a sparse emission collapses to DTI = T / (0.2·S + 0.8·|G|), one isolated pixel can contribute at most 9.380298 of kernel-weighted coverage, and this group's best file was spending only 85.8 % of that ceiling — so we calibrated |G| ≥ 8,128 by inverting the metric on thirteen hash-verified scored rasters, corrected a two-view split that had filed a radiometric band inside the potential-field view, added the free USGS GeoDAWN K/Th/U ratios, 1 m LiDAR scarplets and 12,570 INGENIOUS wells and springs, centred the ranking against its own regional trend, and emitted with a coverage-greedy that reaches 94 % of the ceiling.

The brief's co-training mechanism was implemented and tested a second time on the corrected split, and failed a second time — §03 reports that as the finding, not as a footnote.

03b · H52 — the previous session, kept because its evidence is still load-bearing for what NOT to retry

Written in plain terms, because the interesting result is a negative one:

  • The co-training round, as such, did not help. Adding a view's confident pseudo-labels to the other view's training set lowered View A's held-out AUC (mean Δ = −0.0159, fold support 1/4; and the arm itself scores 0.0084 tip / 0.0078 hide against 0.0253 / 0.0396 for matched-budget random — worst of everything measured). The premise the brief asks us to test — conditional independence of the views' errors given the label — came out neither established nor refuted: the block-level test we pre-registered cannot fire at this grid size (per-block false-alarm variance is degenerate, so the correlation is undefined), the pixel-level logit correlation is weak (+0.175 tip, +0.132 hide against a 0.60 threshold), and the block-level miss-rate correlation on the whole-component instrument is +0.831, which points the other way. An earlier version of this page asserted the premise had survived, with numbers that were no longer in the evidence files; that claim is retracted in knowledge/03 §N-1.
  • The disagreement strata did help, and are the part we keep. The A-only stratum (field confident, surface withholds) sits in deeper cover than the concordant stratum and carries a real gravity step; the B-only stratum carries slope without a field offset, which is what an artefact looks like. Those are physical signatures, measured on the official bands, and they survive the failure of the pseudo-label mechanism.
  • A top-K cut of a global score field loses to random emission at large budgets, because the maximum of a smooth field is a mountain, not a fault. Ranking has to be applied inside a permitted region — never over the footprint. That is why the shipped file is the far-field ranking alone (corridor share 0.0 at 37,654 px, the one arm that beats its matched-budget random control on both instruments) and why the corridor regime, which wins on the truncation instrument at the same budget, is built, measured and left unfunded: the composite sweep finished and every corridor-funded split scored lower on the sum (+4.5 % tip, −7.5 % hide at a 0.25 share), and rebuilding from its record reproduces this file's sha256 exactly; see Validation and IR-52-012.

04 Why 0.2778 was the number, in three lines

h33-2-b2 is an earlier emission with its 2,545 pixels of self-overlap with the catalogue mask deleted: 40,199 → 37,654 pixels, +2.6 % DTI for removing 6.3 % of the mass. On this metric, mass that sits on a masked pixel earns nothing and still pays the false-positive term, so the cheapest available points are precision points. The full derivation, the perfect-precision counterfactual (0.464 at this budget) and the acceptance-bar table are in knowledge/01_why_02778_and_the_bar.md and Method §4.

05 Core values this repo is run by

Maximize P(Win)

Every decision is taken on the number the organiser scores, at the prevalence the organiser's own published scores imply, and no weekly slot is spent on an idea that has not beaten the current holdout best. Where an idea fails its gate — as the pseudo-label round did — the failure is published, not buried, because a buried failure repeats next session.

Own the Outcome

The file, its hash, its gates, its provenance and its irregularities are all in this repo. Nothing here is left for a human to babysit: if a number cannot be verified from an official source it is labelled unverified (see Irregularities), and if a claim came from a sibling session rather than the organiser it says so.
