What the emitted set is actually made of

The pseudo-label branch produced 10,812 whole-segment pixels in total (2,912 A→B, i.e. A confident and B abstaining → candidate buried structure; 7,900 B→A, i.e. B confident and A abstaining → suspect surface artefact). That is 28.7% of the 37,654 emitted pixels. Re-scoring every emitted cell against the same out-of-fold ranks and the same preregistered thresholds (donor ≥ 0.98, receiver in [0.35, 0.65]) splits the final raster like this:

Read that table before reading the download button. Only 1 emitted cell(s) sit in the discovery branch this lane exists to test — the one that would be A-confident and B-abstaining. The bulk of the emission is the 3 px lattice needing to be filled to the 37,654 pixel budget, and 3,191 cells sit in the branch the lane says to distrust. That is a measured result of this round, not a defect in the CSV: it is why the verdict is negative and why no slot was spent.
