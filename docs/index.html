What was tested

The rank field is frozen as F_geo(x) = max(1 − distance / 5 km, 0) around qualified sites. Qualification requires measured discharge temperature ≤30°C, both owner-derived geothermometers ≥100°C and agreement within 20°C. The pre-holdout table audit found 22 qualifying rows at 20 unique raster cells. It is a geochemical hypothesis, not a mapped fault probability.

The comparison refits View A, View B and their max/union on the same pinned inputs and four spatial folds. All arms share the fold's legal emission pool. H58-A emits only where frozen F_geo > 0; support shortfall is reported and cannot be filled with zero-score cells.
