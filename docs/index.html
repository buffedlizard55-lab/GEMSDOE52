What H88 does (the assigned lane)

Co-training between a geophysical view (gravity/magnetic gradients, cover-thickness steps, strain steps, seismicity lineations, conductivity) and a surface view (DEM slope/curvature/edges, radiometric K/Th and total count), with disagreement as the discovery signal — Blum & Mitchell (COLT '98, doi:10.1145/279943.279962).

  • A confident, B abstains → the fault may be buried beneath alluvial cover: these are 78.9 % of the shipped dots. Geological reasoning for every one is in the A-only reasoning CSV.
  • B confident, A abstains → suspect surface artifacts (roads, erosion lines); these cells are vetoed.
  • The output is not the union of the two views: 86 % of dots fall outside the views' top-k union (Jaccard 0.049).
  • Views passed the empirical independence test (max |r| = 0.097 of spatial-block negative errors, abandon threshold 0.60).
