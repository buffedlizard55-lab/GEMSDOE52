01 The one-sentence version

Two models are fitted on the same unlabelled pixels — View A on the gravity, magnetic, conductivity and seismicity layers, View B on slope, curvature, scarp and stream-power from the elevation model — and the useful output is not their agreement, it is where they disagree with conviction: a gravity or magnetic offset with no scarp is a fault under cover, which is exactly the population the LiDAR-derived catalogue is structurally unable to contain.

03 What the holdout said, before the tuning

Written in plain terms, because the interesting result is a negative one:

  • The co-training round, as such, did not help. Adding a view's confident pseudo-labels to the other view's training set lowered View A's held-out AUC in every fold (0.797 → 0.761 on the full grid; see evidence/co_train_folds_*.json). The premise the brief asks us to test — conditional independence of the two views' errors given the label — is measured, and reported, not assumed.
  • The disagreement strata did help, and are the part we keep. The A-only stratum (field confident, surface withholds) sits in deeper cover than the concordant stratum and carries a real gravity step; the B-only stratum carries slope without a field offset, which is what an artefact looks like. Those are physical signatures, measured on the official bands, and they survive the failure of the pseudo-label mechanism.
  • A top-K cut of a global score field loses to random emission at large budgets, because the maximum of a smooth field is a mountain, not a fault. Ranking has to be applied inside a permitted region — never over the footprint. That is why the shipped file is the far-field ranking alone (corridor share 0.0 at 37,654 px, the one arm that beats its matched-budget random control on both instruments) and why the corridor regime, which wins on the truncation instrument at the same budget, is built and measured but left unfunded until the composite sweep finishes; see Validation and IR-52-012.

04 Why 0.2778 was the number, in three lines

h33-2-b2 is an earlier emission with its 2,545 pixels of self-overlap with the catalogue mask deleted: 40,199 → 37,654 pixels, +2.6 % DTI for removing 6.3 % of the mass. On this metric, mass that sits on a masked pixel earns nothing and still pays the false-positive term, so the cheapest available points are precision points. The full derivation, the perfect-precision counterfactual (0.464 at this budget) and the acceptance-bar table are in knowledge/01_why_02778_and_the_bar.md and Method §4.

05 Core values this repo is run by

Maximize P(Win)

Every decision is taken on the number the organiser scores, at the prevalence the organiser's own published scores imply, and no weekly slot is spent on an idea that has not beaten the current holdout best. Where an idea fails its gate — as the pseudo-label round did — the failure is published, not buried, because a buried failure repeats next session.

Own the Outcome

The file, its hash, its gates, its provenance and its irregularities are all in this repo. Nothing here is left for a human to babysit: if a number cannot be verified from an official source it is labelled unverified (see Irregularities), and if a claim came from a sibling session rather than the organiser it says so.
