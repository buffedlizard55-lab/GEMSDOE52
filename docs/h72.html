H72 tested one ranked geological hypothesis

H72-A isolated a geodetic strain/shear View A (`raw_band_04`, `raw_band_07`, `raw_band_08`) against the fixed surface/radiometric View B. It looked for spatially coherent strain and shear with dilatational contrast where View B abstained—a possible concealed structure beneath cover. A broad interseismic signal, survey seam or interpolation edge can mimic this signature. This is a testable geological hypothesis, not fault confirmation.

The H72-A-only holdout was underfilled and final dots failed the lane gate. H72-B through H72-D were ranked proposals only, not tested results. Read the ranked hypothesis review for layers, physical signatures, alternatives, distinctions from repo work and cost.
