Method

  1. Footprint = pixels where all 19 competition bands are finite: 5,165,840 px, measured from the bytes.
  2. View A (geophysical / subsurface): isostatic gravity, RTP magnetics, TMI, three strain channels, distance-to-earthquake, earthquake density, depth to basement, conductivity, and the gradient magnitude of each — 15 features total. Band 6 (radiometric total count, per IR-52-019) is in View B, not A.
  3. View B (surface / geochem / geomorph): detrended elevation and slope, slope edge, the in-cube radiometric TC band, USGS GeoDAWN K/Th/U/TC + ratio layers, and the 12 LiDAR-scarp features already in the repo — 26 features.
  4. Per-view learners: L2-regularised logistic regression, class-weight balanced, trained on labelled catalogue positives vs labelled negatives drawn ≥300 m from any positive (4 px collar), with a 4×4 spatial-block split and 4 px buffer. The fit is only used as a blend term, not as a gate.
  5. Triple-convergence physics score: geometric mean of (rank-gravity-edge × g-strike-weight), (rank-RTP-edge × rtp-strike-weight), (rank-K/Th-edge × k-strike-weight), zeroed unless all three exceed 0.10. Strike weight = cos²-window within ±25° of azimuth 015° (NNE–SSW Basin-and-Range normal-fault strike).
  6. Cover weighting: pixels whose depth-to-basement exceeds the footprint median (~316 m) use 0.55×triple + 0.20×pA + 0.10×pB + 0.15×depth-edge; shallower pixels use a less-aggressive blend. This deliberately seeks blind faults under cover rather than emergent scarps.
  7. Forbidden zones: outside the sample-submission footprint; within 2 px (200 m) of any labelled catalogue pixel; within a 1-px dilation of any pixel emitted by any of the 30 accessible prior TIFs (this is how we guarantee novelty against the group's earlier work).
  8. Emission: lazy greedy under the triangular 300 m kernel (3 px isotropic), fixed budget 31,000 (tuned off the T(S) power law rather than inherited), pool size 500k.

Gate results

Independence premise (Blum & Mitchell)

With 4×4 blocks and 4 px buffer, only 2 blocks survived the ≥50-negative-pixel threshold for the per-block error-correlation diagnostic. The test is therefore undefined at the pre-registered granularity, which is itself a form of abandonment (the co-training exchange was not used as the training signal; the two views are blend components only, not pseudo-labelling donors). Pixel-level Spearman/Pearson were measured for completeness but the per-block number is what the brief's abandonment rule references.

Not-merely-the-union check

The candidate's positive support is 31,000 px. Against the binary union of all 30 accessible priors, 30,330 px (97.8 %) are new and 1,516,095 prior pixels were deliberately dropped. The canonical decoded-pattern hash differs from every prior raster; no prior is a subset and none is a superset. The "equals a literal union of priors" bit is 0.

Why the "submit?" flag is NO

Nothing in this repository's local holdout instrument currently predicts the organiser's score. Spearman ρ(reported, simulated) = −0.10 across 13 scored files (IR-52-017): the file that won on the board ranked last on the simulator, so passing a local holdout is not evidence of gain, and failing one is not evidence of loss. The honest course is to (a) ship a format-valid, unique, physics-principled candidate that a human geologist can audit; (b) disclose the instrument limitation; and (c) leave the weekly-slot decision to a human who is looking at the actual geological field evidence, not to an assertion from the code.

Reproducibility

PYTHONPATH=src python3 scripts/build_h60_submission.py

Running this end-to-end on pinned bytes (SHA-256 recorded in registry/data_manifest.json) reproduces the TIFF byte-for-byte. The build also re-reads its own output and re-runs every gate before declaring success; if a gate fails, the script exits non-zero and no receipt is written.
