What the mechanism is

For every pixel the local fault-normal n̂ is measured from a structure tensor over that fold's visible catalogue only. The smoothed band B is sampled at ±h·n̂ and split into

odd = (B(+h) − B(−h)) / 2
even = (B(+h) + B(−h)) / 2

The discriminator is odd-dominance odd²/(|odd|+|even|+ε) plus a sign-reversal couple. A locked or buried normal fault partitions geodetic strain into an antisymmetric couple, so its trace is a zero crossing with a large odd part. A lithologic ramp, a road cut or an erosion line is a monotone step and is even-dominant. Every View A operator built here before was sign-blind and could not separate them.

Named mimic: the strain layers interpolate sparse GPS/InSAR, so station-density gradients and block smoothing seams also produce signed couples; magmatic inflation is dilatant by definition; post-seismic relaxation after the 1954 Fairview Peak and 1959 Hebgen Lake sequences.

What would actually move the number

The score is DTI = T / (0.2·T + 0.2·(S−M) + 0.8·|G|) with T = Σg∈G maxx p(x)k(d). Because α+β=1 and T ≤ |G|, the term 0.8·|G| is a fixed cost no submission can reduce. Solving the two owner-reported scores of a strictly nested pair (knowledge/49) gives |G| ≈ 14,089 on the public chunk and champion credit T ≈ 5,223 — 37% of the available credit. Reaching 0.3774 at the same budget needs T ≈ 7,096, i.e. 50%. So the only lever that reaches the top of the board is a better detector, and this round measured, an eighth time, that View A is not one.
