Method and the disagreement test

View A · buried structure

Gravity, magnetics, strain, seismicity, conductivity and depth to basement, plus directional semivariance of competition bands 13, 15 and 18.

View B · surface

DEM curvature/slope and radiometrics, plus directional semivariance of bands 12/19 and the new GeoDAWN K, Th, U and total-count channels.

Eight directions at 100–600 m produced anisotropy and local log-variance channels. Spatial-block OOF negative-error max |ρ| was 0.121626, below the 0.60 abandon bar. One buffered exchange donated 7,319 A→B pixels and 7,995 B→A diagnostic pixels, all as whole 50×50-block-contained segments at weight 0.25. Why the owner-attributed 0.2778 file worked

The available bytes show a pruning effect. The 37,654-dot h33-2-b2 is a strict subset of the 44,090-dot, owner-reported 0.2600 d2-8. The 6,436 removed dots all lie 100–200 m from the public catalogue; the retained file starts 223.6 m away. Removing denominator-only mass while retaining credited mass raises DTI = T/(0.2·S + 0.8·|G|) under the separated binary-dot reduction. The nested-score inverse pins |G| near 14,089 under the measured zero-credit-ring condition; a later independent board inversion obtained 14,334.

The file averages roughly 13.87% inferred kernel-credit density, about five times a permitted-footprint uniform control. At the same mass, 0.3195 requires roughly 1.150× its credit and 0.3774 roughly 1.36×. H97 has not demonstrated either increase. The filename-to-0.2778 relation remains owner-reported, not organizer-authenticated.
