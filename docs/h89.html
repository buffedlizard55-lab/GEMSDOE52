What the file actually is

Two learners, two views, and disagreement as the discovery signal — the co-training setup of Blum & Mitchell (COLT '98, pp. 92–100, doi:10.1145/279943.279962).

  • View A (geophysical / subsurface) — 36 channels: magnetics (bands 1,2,3,6,9,14), gravity (5,11,13,18), geodetic strain (4,7,8), seismicity (10,16), depth-to-basement (15), conductivity (17), plus derived gradient/coherence channels and upward-continued TMI.
  • View B (surface) — 37 channels: detrended elevation and slope (12,19), principal curvatures, local relief/roughness, band 6, and the GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th/U ranks and gradients.
  • Discovery direction A→: a pixel is an A-only candidate where View A is confident (rank ≥ 0.90), View B abstains (< 0.60), the modelled sedimentary cover is above the footprint median (319 m), and the A-confident set is continuous (≥ 4 of the 24 neighbours in a 5×5 window). Physically: a basement-cutting fault whose hanging-wall basin has been filled, so no scarp survives at a 100 m cell.
  • Suspicion direction B→: where View B is confident and View A is silent, the pixel is demoted by 0.20·artifact, with artifact = ½(1 − radiometric-edge rank) + ½·valley rank. A fault usually juxtaposes different materials and shows a K/Th/U edge; a drainage incision or a graded road does not.
  • Emission: 33,136 artefact-demoted View-B dots + 4,518 reserved A-only discovery dots, 3 px minimum separation, every pixel more than 200 m from a mapped catalogue trace, binary {0,1}.

Sign convention, measured not assumed: the top 1 % of B_curvature_plus_3 sits at 260 m detrended elevation versus 612 m for the top 1 % of B_curvature_minus_3, so the positive principal curvature is the concave-up (valley) one — which is what the artefact term assumes.

What would falsify this

  • Every A-only dot carries a named non-fault mimic (buried lithological contact or basin-margin palaeochannel) and an explicit falsifier in the reasoning export.
  • The artefact veto itself can delete a real fault wherever aeolian cover or soil mutes the radiometric contrast; that is why it is a −0.20 soft demotion and not a hard mask.
  • The holdout cannot reward sub-cover discovery, so a negative holdout delta is evidence about the instrument as much as about the hypothesis.
