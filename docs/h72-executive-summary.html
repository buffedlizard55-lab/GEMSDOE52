🧪 Methodology

H72-v3 Approach

The v3 submission uses a three-component ranking field:

  1. h19-5 base ranker (60%): The proven powerlaw-budget-multiline-corroborated ranking that scored 0.1922 on the competition. This ranker uses DEM-derived curvature, slope, and multi-line corroboration.
  2. Multi-scale DEM edge coherence (25%): Sobel edge magnitude computed at5 scales (σ=0.5 to 3.0 px, i.e., 50m to 300m), averaged. This captures structural lineaments that persist across spatial scales — a hallmark of fault-controlled features.
  3. LiDAR scarp features (15%): High-resolution (1m source, aggregated to 100m) scarp detection from USGS 3DEP LiDAR data.

All mass is placed more than 200m from any mapped catalogue fault (the "ring mask" that turned 0.2600 into 0.2778 for the champion family). Binary {0,1} mass is metric-optimal.
