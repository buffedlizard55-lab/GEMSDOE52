Exactly how to submit — the three form fields

The DrivenData submission form for this competition has three things to fill in. Here is what each one wants and exactly what to paste.

  1. File to submit. Upload a single-band GeoTIFF, or a ZIP containing exactly one GeoTIFF. Use h76-candidate.tif or h76-candidate.zip — the ZIP holds that one TIFF and nothing else, verified byte-for-byte.
  2. Submission name (must be unique). Every submission needs a name you have not used before. Paste:
    gems76-line-support-B-cotrain-37654px-20261009T193810Z
  3. Note (≤ 140 characters). A short line distinguishing this entry from your others. Paste (137 characters):
    H76 RESEARCH ONLY-DO NOT SUBMIT: co-training trace-integrated View B 600m chord; 37654px binary; >200m off catalogue; lane-feasible c<=50
  1. Open the competition submission page (login required) and upload.
  2. Only do this if the verdict box at the top of this page says the file may be submitted. It currently says DO NOT SUBMIT. This round spends no weekly slot. Download it, read the evidence, and keep your slot.

What you are downloading

A 37,654-cell binary prediction raster produced by co-training View A (potential-field / subsurface: gravity, magnetics, strain, seismicity) against View B (surface: DEM curvature and slope plus the radiometric bands of training_features.tif), following Blum & Mitchell, Combining Labeled and Unlabeled Data with Co-Training, COLT ’98, pp. 92–100, doi:10.1145/279943.279962. The two feature views are disjoint by construction and verified disjoint at load time. Disagreement between them is used to stratify where the budget is spent, never to manufacture training labels.
