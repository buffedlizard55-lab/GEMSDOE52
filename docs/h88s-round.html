1 · The method, as preregistered

Two views of the same ground: View A potential-field and subsurface (gravity and magnetic gradients, strain, basement depth, conductivity), View B surface (DEM-derived curvature and slope, radiometric K/Th/U and their ratios, LiDAR scarp faces). Blum & Mitchell's theorem needs two sufficient and approximately conditionally independent views; the brief makes both premises testable, so both are measured before anything is emitted.

3 · Disagreement strata, and why the shipped field uses A only as a suppressor

Strata are defined by views54.strata(q=0.90) over each fold's allowed set: A_only = A rank high and B rank low; B_only = the reverse; concordant = both high. The brief's discovery signal is A_only. On this instrument the A_only arm scores 0.045017 [0.03365, 0.05692] against single_B's 0.179847, so the shipped file suppresses the B_only stratum and treats A_only as a review queue (each candidate exported with its distance to the mapped catalogue, basement-depth and gravity-gradient channel values, and a written buried-fault reasoning string). B_only suppression is the artefact-avoidance half of the brief: 215312 px in fold 0 alone are surface-confident/A-absent and are exactly the road/canal/erosion-line family.
