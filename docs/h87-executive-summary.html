Geological interpretation and caveats

An A-only gradient could trace a buried structure beneath cover, but intrusive dikes and lithologic contacts are explicit non-fault mimics. The magnetic-gradient percentile can be low for individual emitted cells (it is a weight, not a hard persistence threshold). No cell here is verified as a fault. The GeoDAWN rasters are owner-mirrored and hash-checked, not independently re-downloaded from the official portal or USGS in this sandbox. Band 6 is described as magnetic tilt in its metadata but is measured rank-identical to radiometric total count in the pinned external source; this inconsistency remains flagged.
