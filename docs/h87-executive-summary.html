What H87 actually found

The question. Thirteen files in this repository have owner-reported public-board scores. The metric is exactly linear in the truth density once the cover field of each file is known: si = <g, Mi> / (0.2·Si + 0.8·|G|) with Mi = k(EDT to file i’s dots) and k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0). Thirteen scores are therefore thirteen equations in g, and |G| is one of the unknowns.

Result 1 — a third independent pin on the hidden truth mass. Non-negative least squares on the exact linear form gives |G| = 14,333.8 truth pixels, between H67’s ring-deletion pin (14,088.7 [13,953–14,226]) and the lattice-coverage pin (12,367). Leave-one-out score MAE 0.02007, Spearman 0.9436 over the 13 files.

Result 2 — a negative, and the important one. The fitted mass is FAM_family_consensus 9,938, CAT_catalogue 4,396. Every physical, external and disagreement basis in the model received weight zero: U_uniform_offcat, RING_100_300m, SGMC_offcat, LIDAR_step_coh, RAD_K_high, DIS_A_confident_B_abstains, GRAV_step. A thirteen-basis version of the same fit, run earlier by the same deterministic script, returned an identical solution (log line quoted in evidence/h87_board_inversion.json under extended13_omitted). So the board evidence does not favour gravity steps, LiDAR scarps, radiometrics, seismicity, basement depth, conductivity or either co-training disagreement mask. What it favours is where the owner’s own earlier files were, plus a modest catalogue component.

Result 3 — the budget is derived, not chosen. Adding a dot of exact marginal credit c raises DTI iff c > 0.2·DTI. Emission by that rule self-terminates at 61,427 cells (marginal_rule), median nearest-neighbour spacing 3.1622776601683795 px. This is now a shared tool (gems52.nodes.marginal_greedy) with tests, not a private script.

Calibration of that rule. Under a uniform truth density of the fitted total mass the same tool stops at 24,557 dots, 5.373 px equivalent spacing, predicted DTI 0.1001. The one board score whose geometry is fully known — the organiser-side lattice raster 13gems_20261001_r13-lattice-s5_v2, 206,895 dots, NN median 5.0 px, mean cover 0.3748, cover fraction 0.9984 — is OWNER-REPORTED at 0.0904. The control is within 10.7% of it, which bounds the placement model’s error in the only regime where it can be checked. Two real defects were found and fixed by this control: a stale within-round gain field saturated the 3 px minimum separation (DTI 0.0643 instead of ~0.09), and a raster-order tie-break produced a square packing where the pinned raster is staggered (~8% of achievable DTI).

Geological reasoning for the A-only candidates

3,424 emitted cells are described row by row in the reasoning CSV. Of those, 3,424 (5.6%) are A-only: View A (potential field and subsurface — gravity gradient/slope/horizontal-gradient, magnetics tmi_hg+tmi_vg+|rtp|, geodetic second invariant + shear + dilatation, seismicity density minus distance, basement-depth step, surface conductivity) ranks the cell in its top 15% while View B (surface — DEM slope and curvature, LiDAR step/excess/laplacian-negative/coherence, radiometric K and Th, band 6 total count) abstains between the 35th and 65th percentile. That is the co-training disagreement the brief asks to pseudo-label: a coherent subsurface edge with no surface expression, which is what a fault buried under alluvial cover looks like. Each such row carries the named non-fault process that could mimic it (basin-margin facies step, dyke or intrusive contact, geodetic interpolation seam, palaeo-channel under cover) and three measured distances: to the catalogue (2699.4 m mean), to the nearest derived SGMC fault (1456.0 m median) and to the nearest thermal well or spring (1,432 cells within 500 m).

The co-training lane itself is closed by pre-registered gates in this repository: view independence passes (max |rho| 0.1333 over 2,089 blocks against a 0.60 bar) but View A sufficiency is refuted seven times (mean out-of-fold AUC 0.5113). H87 therefore ships the transparent rank composites and their disagreement masks as reasoning and ranking inputs, and does not re-open a pseudo-label exchange. See registry/h77cond_preregistration.json and evidence/h71_*.

Limitations, stated before anyone acts on this

  • The density is mostly a mirror. 9,938 of 14,334 mass units sit on the mean cover of the owner’s own scored ladder, and that ladder is nested (the 0.2778 file is a strict subset of the 0.2600 file, which is a subset of the 0.2477 file). A leave-one-out basis rebuilt from the other six members of a nested family is strongly collinear with the target it has to predict, so the leave-one-out MAE 0.02007 overstates how much the model would know about a file it had never seen. The pin on |G| is robust (it is a scale, and two independent methods agree); the shape of the fitted density is not.
  • The holdout instrument does not predict the board here (Spearman −0.10 across this repository’s scored files, IR-H77-005): it hides catalogue faults while the board scores faults the catalogue lacks. A HOLDOUT-DTI below random is therefore not proof the candidate is bad on the board, and a value above random would not be proof it is good. It is the only unbiased instrument available, so it decides promotion and it says no.
  • Truth realisations are not truth. The PREDICTED-BOARD column samples |G| pixels with probability proportional to the fitted density and scores exactly against them. It answers “is this pattern consistent with the density that explains the board?”, not “what will this score?”.
  • Two byte patterns share the score 0.1563 in the owner-reported registry, so attribution of a score to a file is not organiser-confirmed anywhere in this round.
  • |G| enters the acceptance bar. If the true mass is 12,367 rather than 14,334, the bar 0.2·DTI is higher and the self-terminated budget is smaller; the whole pinned interval [12,300, 14,100] should be swept before any promotion decision.
  • Not tested: a density fitted with the family basis excluded (the sandbox OOM-killed the 13-basis run before that ablation could be added), any |G| sweep of the emission, and any organiser upload.
