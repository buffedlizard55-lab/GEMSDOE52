What was tested

View A contained 60 label-free directional-variogram features derived only from in-stack bands 2, 13, 15, 17, 7, and 16: reduced-to-pole magnetics, isostatic gravity, modelled basement depth, surface conductivity, geodetic shear, and earthquake intensity. View B contained 19 shared-cache raw and DEM-derived features from bands 6, 12, and 19; band 6's source/tag identity conflict remains open, so its use is confined to View B and treated as provisional. Their feature lists are disjoint. H87 re-tested the DVA transform as a strictly geophysical teacher; it does not claim the transform itself is new.

A directional-insensitive single-feature leakage canary peaked at 0.6543, below the 0.90 alarm. The two OOF error metrics were weakly correlated on catalogue-zero proxies (MSE Pearson/Spearman 0.0636/0.0782; FPR −0.0135/0.0666), but a low correlation is not proof of conditional independence. The combined transfer gate required both this check and view sufficiency. View A failed, so no pseudo labels were exchanged.

The candidate is an A-high / B-abstaining field. Its named non-fault mimics include lithologic or basin-fill boundaries, inversion/gridding seams, conductive clays or saline fill, and modelled basement edges. Candidate locations are review targets only, not verified faults, vents, or geothermal systems.
