2 · What H88 tested, in one paragraph

Blum & Mitchell's co-training needs two views that are each sufficient for the task and approximately conditionally independent, then uses their disagreement to find the most informative unlabelled examples. Per the brief, View A is potential-field/subsurface and View B is surface (DEM curvature/slope + the radiometric band in the stack). This repository's nine prior rounds showed View B works (mean held-out-region AUC 0.68–0.70) while View A sits at chance when fed raw amplitudes. H88 attacked that directly: it replaced View A's representation with directional variogram anisotropy — an 8-direction semivariance fan reduced to (max−min)/(max+min) and log10(mean) — on bands never used for it before: 2 rtp (reduced-to-pole magnetics), 9 tmi_vg (magnetic vertical gradient), 13 iso_grav_anom (isostatic gravity). View B: 12 det_elev, 19 det_elev_slope, 6 (radiometric total count by bytes — IR-H88-003). The discovery signal is the disagreement field pct_rank(A) − pct_rank(B). The disagreement is used as the brief's selection/emission signal; the separate pseudo-label exchange form of the lane was measured in H71 and lowered View A, so no exchange was run this round.

3 · Why 0.2778 scores what it scores (the competition arithmetic)

DTI = T / (0.2·(T+S−M) + 0.8·|G|), where T = kernel-weighted credit on true fault pixels, S = emitted mass, M = mass inside known fault kernels (which the organizer masks out), |G| = total ground-truth mass. Solving the two known dotted reference points (37,654 dots → 0.2778; 44,090 dots → 0.2600) gives |G| ≈ 14,088.7 px and T ≈ 5,223.1 px: the champion earns credit on ~13.9 % of its emitted mass, and 99.26 % of that mass sits outside every mapped fault kernel — it wins on faults the public catalogue lacks. Beating 0.3195 requires either +15.0 % credit density (T ≥ 6,007 at S = 37,654) or −32.6 % mass (S ≤ 25,384 at today's credit). Placement is already within ~4 % of its geometric optimum and the metric's marginal rule caps useful distance at ≈282 m, so the −33 % route is closed; the +15 % route needs a detector that ranks off-catalogue faults, which no round has yet produced. Full treatment: knowledge/82_why_02778_and_the_route_above_it.md.
