What is this submission?

H59 is a unique approach that has not been submitted before. It combines:

  • Multi-scale potential-field edge detection (gravity + magnetics at 3 scales)
  • Co-training disagreement between geophysical and surface views
  • Fault-tip structural continuation
  • Metric-aware greedy placement

It is 98.8% novel compared to the previous best submission (H33, scored 0.2778), with only 466 overlapping pixels out of 37,654.
