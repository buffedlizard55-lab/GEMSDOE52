What is in the file (one paragraph)

The candidate has two arms. A core of 25517 pixels is the exact intersection of two scored files whose credit is bounded by their published scores (4,168 to 5,223 truth-pixel credits), entirely more than 200 m from the mapped catalogue. A novel arm of 15000 pixels was selected outside the union of all 33 accessible aligned prior rasters and at least three pixels from the core, ordered by along-strike continuation of the credited cloud and by the 3 m DEM scarp gates and 1 m LiDAR step evidence. Every emitted cell is an isolated single pixel; the arm size was chosen by budget_rule to maximise the probability of beating 0.278 over the exact core-credit interval and a stated novel-density prior of 0.03–0.14 credits per cell.
