Part 1 — Exactly how to submit a file to the competition

1
Download the GeoTIFF. Download .tif Download .zip The .zip contains exactly the one GeoTIFF and is accepted interchangeably with the .tif.
2
Open the submission page. DrivenData → DOE GEMS → Submissions (login required) and click New submission.
3
File to submit — click "No file chosen" and select the downloaded .tif (or the .zip). The form accepts "a single-band GeoTIFF (.tif) file, or a .zip file containing a single GeoTIFF" and it "must match the submission format's CRS, shape, and geotransform". Our file is single-band float32, EPSG:32611, 3730×3292 cells of 100 m, transform (100, 0, 243350, 0, −100, 4508550) — identical to the organiser's sample_submission.tif grid.
4
Note (optional) — "A short comment to help you or your team tell submissions apart later e.g. clustering with k=25". Paste:
H88 bidir co-train A/B disagreement, cover-step ViewA, 25400px mass lever, 3px, 200m collar; HOLDOUT-DTI below bar, research candidate
5
Create submission and wait for the receipt. Copy the score from the submission page back into the repository's receipts as ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED — that is the only label allowed on a real score.

Part 2 — What H88 is and what was measured

Hypothesis (frozen before any fit, registry/h88_preregistration.json). In a two-view co-training setup (Blum & Mitchell, COLT '98, doi:10.1145/279943.279962), disagreement is the discovery signal: where the geophysical view (gravity/magnetic gradients, cover-thickness steps, strain steps, seismicity lineations, conductivity) is confident and the surface view (DEM slope/curvature/edges, radiometric K/Th and total count) abstains, a fault may be buried beneath alluvial cover and missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS surface-trace catalogue. Where the surface view is confident and the geophysical view abstains, the lineament is suspected to be a surface artifact (road, erosion line) and is vetoed.

Named non-fault mimics (pre-registered): lithologic contacts and intrusive margins (potential-field edges without offset); basin-margin facies steps (cover steps without faulting); paleo-channels; aftershock clusters; road cuts and erosion lines. Every buried-dominant dot carries a reasoning row naming which components fired and these alternatives: A-only reasoning CSV (20,050 rows).

Independence test (the Blum–Mitchell assumption, measured): spatial-block out-of-fold error correlation of the two views on labelled negatives is r = 0.0965 (max |r|, 50 px blocks), far below the 0.60 abandon threshold — the method was not abandoned. This is weak proxy-negative correlation, not proof of conditional independence.

Hide-and-recover HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator gems52-pooled-hide-v1; 60,894 withheld positive pixels; whole fault segments withheld with an 80 px buffer; visible catalogue masked pixel-exactly; 200 m collar from visible faults only; 9,400 dots per fold per arm; 95 % paired 20 km cluster bootstrap, 1,000 draws):

Interpretation. On hide-and-recover of catalogue faults the H88 disagreement field performs at the random floor (paired delta vs random −0.0040, CI [−0.0163, +0.0095]), far below the promotion bar 0.192829. That is the honest negative result, and it matches the repository's measured finding that this family's holdout and the public board are uncorrelated (Spearman ≈ −0.10): the board rewards faults the catalogue lacks, while this instrument hides faults the catalogue has. The value of the round is the completed test: bidirectional disagreement with cover-step / strain-step / seismicity-lineation channels does not beat random on catalogue recovery, so the next round should graft the disagreement arm onto the strong surface channels (H82/H84 DVA2/HVA, holdout 0.19) instead of replacing them.

Uniqueness and lane. The raster is distinct from 147 registry rasters (decoded-pattern uniqueness: evidence/h88_uniqueness.json); surface rank-correlation vs every registry raster ≤ 0.091 (bar 0.90); final dots: see evidence/h88_lane_dots.json. It is not the union of the two views (86 % of dots outside the views' spaced top-k union; Jaccard 0.049).

Mass lever. The shipped file uses S = 25,400 dots (−33 % vs the champion's 37,654) with the champion's free-precision move: the ≤ 200 m ring around the mapped catalogue is deleted, because those pixels can never earn discovery credit while still paying the false-positive tax.
