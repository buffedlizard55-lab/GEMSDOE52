What the reported H33 result does—and does not—show

The owner-reported H33 filename/score association is not tied to an organizer submission-page receipt. The public board confirms a team-level row only. The repository’s byte audit records H33 as a strict subset of its reported parent, with 6,436 cells removed and none added; the removed cells were 100–200 m from the known-catalogue traces in the owner-mirrored raster audit. This pattern is consistent with selective thinning of marginal predictions.

Correction: do not say the deleted ring had exactly zero credit or that the organizer masks a 200 m buffer. Private new-fault truth may lie within 300 m of known traces, so the credit of those deleted cells cannot be inferred from the catalogue alone. The improvement is a plausible precision/selection effect, not proof of a better detector or a geological no-fault zone. The official metric uses a triangular 300 m kernel with α=0.2 and β=0.8; false positives are not free.
