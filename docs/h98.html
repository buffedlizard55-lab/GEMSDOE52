Why the two screens disagree, and what that means

The screening instrument's truth is SGMC-mapped fault pixels ≥300 m from the competition catalogue; the validation instrument's truth is the competition catalogue itself, hidden a quadrant at a time. The same arm scores +0.0576 against one and −0.0189 against the other. Both numbers are real and both are kept. The honest reading is that this arm does not survive a change of truth population, which is the same instrument-dependence this repository has measured all round (knowledge/76 §6) — and it is the reason no H98 file is emitted.
