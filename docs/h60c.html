1 · Why h33-h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778

h33-2-b2 is gems24-d2-8 (reported 0.2600) with 6,436 pixels deleted. Every one of them lies within 100–200 m of the mapped USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue (measured: A \ B = 0, B \ A = 6,436, and the champion's minimum distance to the catalogue is 223.6 m). Inverting the published metric on that nested pair gives the deleted ring's credit as exactly zero and yields |G| = 14,088.7 px — the size of the hidden expert-drawn truth. Deleting 14.6 % of the file's mass raised the score by +6.8 %. That is the whole story: the champion is not a better detector, it is a file that stopped paying tax on pixels that can never earn credit.

The catalogue itself is 60,988 px (1.18 % of the 5,167,373-px footprint); the hidden truth is 0.273 % of it. Publication rounding to four decimals moves |G| by only ±70 px.

4 · The marginal rule that sizes the emission

A pixel with expected incremental credit c raises DTI iff c > α·DTI/(1 − α·DTI). At DTI ≈ 0.32 that is c > 0.0684 — about 2.7× uniform random (measured 0.024–0.028). The core clears this by 2.4–3× and ships whole; every arm pixel is admitted only on that basis.

Dot spacing. On the 100 m integer lattice, sampling a straight trace every 3 px returns 2.333 credit per dot and covers 77.8 % of the trace; the champion's d2-8 (2.8 px) returns 2.147 and covers 76.7 %. Spacing 3 strictly dominates 2.8 — fewer dots, more coverage — so the whole H60C emission uses 3 px.

5 · The two-view co-training the brief requires

Independence test (Blum–Mitchell premise). Per 50×50 px block over labelled negatives (outside the catalogue and a 4 px buffer), the Spearman correlation of the two views' out-of-fold confident-positive rates is 0.1175 across 2128 blocks, against the preregistered abandonment threshold 0.6. The premise is not refuted at this granularity.

Disagreement strata. {'a_only': 58563, 'b_only': 75211, 'concordant': 43668, 'neither': 4691536} — A-only pixels are where the potential-field view is confident and the surface view abstains, i.e. subsurface structure with no scarp, the signature of a fault buried beneath cover and exactly the class a surface-expression catalogue misses. B-only is the artefact suspect (road cut, erosion line, graded fan margin) and is reported, not emitted.

View definitions. View A = potential field and subsurface: bands 1, 2, 3, 9, 14 (magnetics), 13, 11, 18, 5 (isostatic gravity), 4, 7, 8 (geodetic strain), 10, 16 (seismicity), 15 (depth to basement), 17 (surface conductivity). View B = surface: bands 12, 19 (detrended elevation and its slope) and band 6. Band 6 is described by the organiser as a magnetic tilt derivative but measured on the bytes ranges 2.95–88.6, which is a radiometric total count (IR-52-019 / IR-52-034). It is the only radiometric band in the training raster, so it belongs in the surface view; leaving it in the potential-field view would corrupt the very independence test this method has to pass.

6 · A preregistered hypothesis that failed

The corroboration ladder — credit density = ρ0 · rclade(c−1) · rextf, four coefficients fitted to the twelve measured credits — does not reproduce them: median |relative error| 0.219, maximum 0.827, leave-one-file-out median 0.281. Corroboration count is therefore not a sufficient statistic for credit density, which is the same conclusion knowledge/03 N-10 reached by a different route ("habitat is not credit"). Recorded as a refutation, not smoothed away.
