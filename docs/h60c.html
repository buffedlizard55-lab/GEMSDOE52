4 · Conditional metric algebra and geometric diagnostics

Conditional metric algebra, not measured hidden-truth credit. A pixel with expected incremental credit c raises DTI iff c > α·DTI/(1 − α·DTI). At DTI ≈ 0.32 that is c > 0.0684 under the stated algebraic setup. This is a threshold identity, not evidence that the former core/arm split clears the competition score bar.

Dot spacing. On the 100 m integer lattice, sampling a straight trace every 3 px gives 2.333 units of idealized kernel-footprint coverage per dot and covers 77.8 % of a straight trace; the local d2-8 bitmap has 2.8 px spacing with 2.147 units and 76.7 % coverage. This geometric comparison does not estimate hidden-truth credit or public score.

5 · The two-view co-training the brief requires

Independence test (Blum–Mitchell premise). Per 50×50 px block over labelled negatives (outside the catalogue and a 4 px buffer), the Spearman correlation of the two views' out-of-fold confident-positive rates is 0.1175 across 2128 blocks, against the preregistered abandonment threshold 0.6. The premise is not refuted at this granularity.

Disagreement strata. {'a_only': 58563, 'b_only': 75211, 'concordant': 43668, 'neither': 4691536} — A-only pixels are where the potential-field view is confident and the surface view abstains, i.e. subsurface structure with no scarp, which is compatible with a buried-fault hypothesis but is not fault-specific and is not confirmed by this catalogue proxy. B-only is the artefact suspect (road cut, erosion line, graded fan margin) and is reported, not emitted.

View definitions. View A = potential field and subsurface: bands 1, 2, 3, 9, 14 (magnetics), 13, 11, 18, 5 (isostatic gravity), 4, 7, 8 (geodetic strain), 10, 16 (seismicity), 15 (depth to basement), 17 (surface conductivity). View B = surface: bands 12, 19 (detrended elevation and its slope) and band 6. Band 6 is described by the organiser as a magnetic tilt derivative but measured on the bytes ranges 2.95–88.6, which is a radiometric total count (IR-52-019 / IR-52-034). It is the only radiometric band in the training raster, so it belongs in the surface view; leaving it in the potential-field view would corrupt the very independence test this method has to pass.
