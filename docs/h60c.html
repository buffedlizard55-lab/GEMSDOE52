1 · Conditional reconstruction from the owner-reported 0.2778 / 0.2600 pair

The file-to-score mapping for h33-h33-2-b2 (reported 0.2778) is owner-reported, not organizer-confirmed; the source repository says that no organizer score is recorded. On the owner-mirrored bytes, this raster is nested inside gems24-d2-8 (reported 0.2600) with 6,436 cells removed, near the mapped USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue. Conditional on the file mapping, score values and metric assumptions being correct, that rounded pair is consistent with a score increase after removing predictions. It does not prove that every deleted cell earned exactly zero credit, that the underlying hidden-truth size is known, or that the score attribution is authentic.

The often-quoted |G| ≈ 14,089 px is a conditional estimate from rounded owner-reported values, not a measurement from organizer labels. The repository's catalogue count and raster footprint are byte-derived; they do not authenticate any hidden-truth count or public score association.

4 · A marginal-value sensitivity rule (not an observed score)

Under the assumed metric accounting, a pixel with expected incremental credit c raises DTI iff c > α·DTI/(1 − α·DTI). At an assumed DTI of about 0.32, the algebra gives c > 0.0684. The 0.024–0.028 random comparison is a historical simulation diagnostic, not an organizer result. These values depend on the simplified accounting assumptions and do not validate the H60C arm.

Dot spacing. On the 100 m integer lattice, sampling a straight trace every 3 px returns 2.333 credit per dot and covers 77.8 % of the trace; the champion's d2-8 (2.8 px) returns 2.147 and covers 76.7 %. Spacing 3 strictly dominates 2.8 — fewer dots, more coverage — so the whole H60C emission uses 3 px.

5 · Archived H60C two-view co-training diagnostics

Dependence diagnostics, not proof of conditional independence. The primary archived value rho_block_mean_oof_probability = 0.1175 is a rank correlation of block-mean out-of-fold score levels on catalogue-zero pixels, not a correlation of per-pixel OOF errors. The receipt also reports block false-positive-rate correlations. Weak correlations on these proxies do not establish sufficient views or conditional independence. CTD5 has a separate negative-error diagnostic, but its legacy holdout is invalid for promotion; see the current CTD5 run card.

Disagreement strata (model-defined, not verified geology). {'a_only': 58563, 'b_only': 75211, 'concordant': 43668, 'neither': 4691536}. A-only is compatible with a buried structure but is not evidence of a confirmed fault; lithologic contact or basin-fill density contrast can mimic it. B-only may reflect a road cut, erosion line or fan margin and is reported, not emitted.

View definitions. View A = potential field and subsurface: bands 1, 2, 3, 9, 14 (magnetics), 13, 11, 18, 5 (isostatic gravity), 4, 7, 8 (geodetic strain), 10, 16 (seismicity), 15 (depth to basement), 17 (surface conductivity). View B = surface: bands 12, 19 (detrended elevation and its slope) and provisionally band 6. The band-6 radiometric interpretation comes from owner-mirror byte comparisons and prior contractor-document review; its primary identity and units remain unresolved in this checkout.

6 · A preregistered hypothesis that failed

The conditional corroboration ladder — inferred credit density = ρ0 · rclade(c−1) · rextf, four coefficients fitted to the twelve credit estimates derived from owner-reported score labels — does not reproduce them: median |relative error| 0.219, maximum 0.827, leave-one-file-out median 0.281. Corroboration count is therefore not a sufficient statistic for credit density, which is the same conclusion knowledge/03 N-10 reached by a different route ("habitat is not credit"). Recorded as a refutation, not smoothed away.
