Buried Basin-and-Range faults,
where three edges line up at NNE.

H60 searches for the blind normal faults that the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue misses because they die out beneath alluvial cover. It requires three independent geophysical contrasts — isostatic-gravity step, reduced-to-pole magnetic step, and potassium/thorium radiometric edge — to coincide within a 300 m disc, all oriented along the dominant NNE–SSW Basin-and-Range strike (~15° east of north), with a down-thrown-basin signature in depth-to-basement. It is not a re-dotting of existing scarps. Download is one click above; the file is format-legal, range-legal and 97.8 % novel against the accessible inventory of prior submissions from this group.

Hypothesis, in one paragraph

Testable hypothesis, not an established fact: some unmapped normal faults may be concealed beneath valley fill, where surface-scarp detectors have limited direct expression. The 0.2778-to-file association remains owner-reported and unauthenticated (knowledge/49). But a blind range-bounding fault still (i) offsets the isostatic gravity field across the contact, (ii) offsets the reduced-to-pole magnetic field where basement is magnetic, (iii) juxtaposes radiometrically distinct bedrock on the two sides (visible as a K/Th ratio step where cover is thin or dissected), and (iv) shows a depth-to-basement gradient as the sedimentary wedge thickens toward the down-thrown block. Requiring all four to line up at the Basin-and-Range strike discriminates against isolated gravity intrusions, cultural magnetic noise, roads and ridge-top erosion that defeat any single channel. See full audit.

What's new relative to prior rounds (30 prior TIFs checked)

Triple AND-gate, not OR

grav × RTP × K/Th

H57/H59 used max(pA, pB); H55/H19/H28 used a single scarp or surface field. H60 demands that three independent physics families co-edge at the same pixel. Co-located evidence from these channels could reduce single-channel confounding by roads, lava-flow margins or gravity intrusions, but does not rule those mimics out; the claim requires holdout and geological validation.

Strike-gated

15° ± 25°

Only edges whose gradient direction lies within ±25° of the dominant Basin-and-Range normal-fault azimuth (NNE–SSW, ~015°) contribute. E–W and NW–SE artefacts (cultural, volcanic) are suppressed at the door.

Cover-seeking

depth > median

The novel arm is restricted to pixels with above-median depth to basement (cover deeper than ~316 m in this footprint) — exactly the A-only stratum H57 labelled as "buried" but ranked worst when fed as a plain logistic score. The AND-gate plus strike restriction is what rehabilitates that population.
