Official sources and dated clarifications

These are links to reviewable official sources, with observation dates retained in the evidence notes. They do not authenticate the repository's owner-maintained data mirrors. The current interpretation of the known-fault mask and 0.2778 evidence is summarized in knowledge/49.

  • Problem description (page 967) — the distance-weighted Tversky index verbatim (α=0.2, β=0.8, k(d)=max(1−d/R,0), R=300 m=3 px), the four submission-format clauses, the two-round prize structure, and the sentence that decides this family's whole strategy: the test faults are “not contained within the current public USGS database”, and “portions of the existing fault data may be misaligned from the true location of the surface fault, which is the prediction target”.
  • Public leaderboard — the saved 2026-10-09 20:18 UTC observation records top row xiaofanhu 0.3774/rank 1, DARD 0.3195/rank 7, and extradr19 0.2778/rank 17. These are team-level board values without TIFF hashes or score receipts; the 0.2778 row is not the highest public score. See evidence/leaderboard_observation_2026-10-09T201800Z.json.
  • DrivenData staff scoring clarification, forum 11516/4: the known-fault mask is pixel-exact and identical to the provided training labels; only new-fault truth is scored; new-fault truth may occur within 300 m of a known trace. Thus distance to a known trace alone does not imply zero credit or an automatic penalty. A prediction that misses new-fault truth can still be penalized under the metric. See knowledge/49; this does not explain the owner-reported 0.2778 attribution or a score change.
  • Forum 11550 — leaderboard aggregation staff, 2026-10-01: “pooling over all pixels in the public subset and computing a single Tversky index”; chunking undisclosed; “the final re-evaluation will be on the entire GeoDAWN area”. Confirms the repo's pooled-DTI algebra and makes the budget round-dependent (knowledge/26 H60-F).
  • Forum 11527 — how were the new test faults identified? staff, 2026-09-23: data sources, fault types and coverage are not shared; and “the largest prize pool (Phase 2) will use a test set that is updated by expert review of all Phase 1 submissions, so your fault predictions have an impact on final evaluation even if they are not the most performant in Phase 1”.
  • Forum 11557 — units of deq_n100a15 and meaning of ieq_n100a15 staff, 2026-10-07: both are “the INGENIOUS earthquake rate density layers … clipped to the GeoDAWN area and re-gridded to 100m”, with the upstream detail in eq_rate_density_details.docx inside “Earthquake Density Models.zip” at the INGENIOUS GDR submission 1391. Closes IR-R4-002; obtainability of that archive was not tested from this sandbox (gdr.openei.org is not on its allowed host list) and is not claimed.
