Leakage and independence checks

Original connected fault components were held out whole, with an 80-pixel Euclidean training buffer; transform support is 36 pixels. The visible catalogue is masked pixel-exactly. All catalogue-derived sampling constraints use the fold-visible catalogue.

HOLDOUT-DTI diagnostics (not DTI scores): maximum feature-alone AUC 0.668330, below the .90 alarm; maximum negative-error correlation 0.098507 across 2,085 blocks, below the .60 stop threshold. Catalogue-zero negatives are not verified fault absence. Neither screen proves conditional independence or sufficient views.

One whole-segment confident-to-abstaining exchange was run, with training-only pseudo-labels and no evaluation overlap. No further hypothesis or parameter search was attempted after the negative result.

Why did the reported H33 result improve?

The supplied 0.2778 is OWNER-REPORTED / NOT ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The source page currently says no organizer score exists. We did not obtain a receipt that resolves that conflict.

The files themselves establish a simple operation: 40,199 → 37,654 dots, deleting 2,545 off-catalogue cells within 200 m of the visible catalogue, adding none. Removing low-value mass can improve DTI if reduced false-positive weight outweighs lost maximum-cover credit. Pixel-exact masked known pixels do not incur penalties; these removed flank cells were off that mask.

Pruning improves DTI only if:
lost_credit × (1 − 0.2 × old_DTI)
  < 0.2 × old_DTI × removed_FP_mass

Without hidden truth and authenticated paired receipts, we cannot recover those terms, prove zero ring credit, infer hidden-label count, or guarantee a better score. CTD5 offers no supported claim of beating the reported result. Recomputed set-relation receipt.

Limitations and next work, in order

  1. Resolve universal-coverage registry entries prospectively in the shared selector—or stop before spending another training budget. No private threshold exception.
  2. Authenticate core input provenance and obtain an organizer score-to-file receipt. A hash match to an owner mirror does not authenticate its upstream source.
  3. Verify band-6 identity and modeled-layer lineage from official metadata. Remote USGS/GDR/DrivenData hosts were restricted here.
  4. Pre-register a genuinely support-capacity-feasible comparison. Do not reinterpret this budget-incomparable result as a matched-budget win.
  5. Use independent geological validation before calling any candidate a fault, much less a geothermal resource.
