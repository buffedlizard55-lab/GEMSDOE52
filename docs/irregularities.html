IR-52-001 There is no radiometric band. Notes in sibling repos of this family describe features from “gamma-ray” channels. No such channel exists in the provided data. Band inventory read from the organiser's own data README, published as data/band_inventory.json: 19 bands, all gravity / magnetic / conductivity / elevation / seismicity / depth-to-basement. grep -l radiometric across the raw data returns nothing. mitigated — any radiometric feature is excluded by construction, and the feature builder is checked against the inventory in scripts/prepare_data.py.

IR-52-005 sample_submission.tif is not empty. It ships 60,988 positive pixels, and every one of them sits on a labelled fault pixel. Counted from the file. Copying it would be both a duplicate submission and a metric own-goal: the organiser masks the catalogue pixels, so mass there earns nothing and the file would also fail the uniqueness gate. mitigated — gates.uniqueness_report fails any candidate whose mass is a subset of a prior, and emission is computed on valid & ~catalogue.
IR-52-006 The page text and the portal validator contradict each other about no-data encoding. The rules say “null or NaN where there is no data”; the validator enforces 0 <= v <= 1, and NaN fails that comparison whatever the direction. The historical “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” rejection in this family is exactly this mechanism, and grid.write_geotiff independently refuses non-finite pixels. mitigated — empty space is written as 0.0; NaN anywhere is now a named gate failure. Scoring is identical either way (p = 0 contributes nothing to FPw), so nothing is lost.

!1Open risks we are carrying into the file, deliberately

  • Placement is the whole game and our holdout is a proxy. The metric's perfect-precision counterfactual at this budget is 0.464 while every arm we measure is at 0.03–0.05 on the instruments, because the instruments hold out a fraction of the truth population at a far higher prevalence. Only differences at matched budget transfer. Anyone reading 0.0518 as a score forecast is misreading this site.
  • Phase 2 truth is a revision of Phase 1. The staff position (community thread, 23 Sep, #7) is that no details of the test faults are given and the final pool is built after expert review of all Phase 1 submissions — so a public-board score is not a bound on the private one. Our mitigation is the 1,542-row per-segment reasoning table shipped next to the raster: if a reviewer disagrees, the disagreement is locatable.
  • The weekly limit is the binding constraint, not compute. Our board row shows 10 submissions used. Nothing on this site has been uploaded on our initiative, and the workflow is explicitly forbidden from publishing a new raster by itself.
