1. Hypothesis, mechanism, and the process that mimics it

Hypothesis. Fault damage zones impose a direction-dependent semivariance on isostatic gravity and deterministic-elevation fields that the 4-direction H75 fan cannot resolve; extending the fan to 8 integer directions at lags 100-600 m and testing each pixel's winning direction against the fold's visible-catalogue strike recovers oblique and transfer structures that are absent from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue.

Mechanism. A 300-900 m damage zone of increased fracture porosity and brecciation perturbs the Bouguer field and the regolith thickness along the fault's own strike. Semivariance measured parallel to that strike stays low while the perpendicular lag crosses the contrast, so gamma(h, phi) is anisotropic with the maximum perpendicular to the fault. An 8-direction fan resolves the perpendicular at 22.5 degrees instead of 45, and cos(2*(theta_max - psi - pi/2)) tests whether the winning direction is consistent with the regional and local strike field measured from the fold's own visible catalogue.

Named non-fault mimic. Named non-fault process that produces the same signature: linear drainage incision and road cuts align with the maximum-slope direction and imprint a directional semivariance anomaly of their own; range-front bajada edges and lithologic contacts (especially the Tertiary volcanic/basalt flow margins visible in band 15 depth_to_base_surf) produce a gravity gradient with a preferred direction and no fault. Every emitted cell carries its own row in the reasoning CSV naming which of these it could be and the falsifier.

Co-training lane status. not re-run as pseudo-label exchange: View-A sufficiency has failed six consecutive rounds (knowledge/55, knowledge/64, evidence/h74_sufficiency.json). The lane's mandated independence test is reported as a standing measurement instead, and the A-only arm is fitted and scored here so the failure is re-measured rather than cited.

7. Why the best OWNER-REPORTED file in this repository scored 0.2778

The algebra is in knowledge/49 and was verified against the organiser’s published metric text. With α = 0.2, β = 0.8 and a triangular kernel of R = 300 m,

DTI = T / (0.2·T + 0.2·(S−M) + 0.8·|G|)

where T is weighted true-positive mass, S the number of emitted pixels, M the mass the catalogue already covers, and |G| the weighted ground-truth mass. For well-separated dots this collapses to DTI ≈ T / (0.2·S + 0.8·|G|), so what a submission really buys is the hit rate ρ = T/S. Two facts follow:

  1. β = 0.8 makes recall expensive and precision cheap. Every emitted pixel costs 0.2 of a unit; every missed ground-truth unit costs 0.8. So the optimum is a sparse binary raster placed where the hit rate is highest, not a smooth probability field. Binary {0,1} is optimal because the metric is linear in p.
  2. The 0.2778 file is the 0.2600 file with its 100–200 m catalogue ring deleted. Those 826 shared pixels show the overlap. Removing 6,436 px that sat inside the mapped catalogue earned zero credit (the ground truth was already covered) while still paying the full false-positive tax: +6.8% relative. That is the single largest measured lever in this repository, and it is a placement lever, not a modelling lever.

The champion’s implied ρ is about 0.1387, roughly 5.0× the random-placement rate of 0.0279. To reach 0.3195 at S = 37,654 needs ρ = 0.1595; at S = 100,000 it needs only ρ = 0.0999. The binding constraint is therefore the decay of ranker quality with budget, ρ(S), not the choice of S. H82 attacks exactly that: better ranking per emitted pixel. Nothing on this page claims that it succeeded.
