5 H54 · what replaced the broken instrument

The instruments above are still reported, unchanged, because deleting a failed measurement hides it. But they no longer select anything. knowledge/10 records why: the whole-component hide simulator's premise is that the hidden truth is a held-out part of the mapped catalogue, and the bytes say the hidden truth does not come within 200 m of the mapped catalogue at all.

What selects now is arithmetic on artefacts the organiser has already scored. 6 The budget rule, as a probability

DTI = (t_core + ρ_novel·n_novel) / (0.2·S + 0.8·|G|), capped by T ≤ |G|. Two unknowns, treated differently: t_core is bounded exactly, so it gets a uniform prior over that exact interval; ρ_novel is unknowable, so it gets a uniform prior from "no better than uniform random" (2.79 % credit per emitted pixel, measured) to "as good as the champion's own average" (13.87 %, measured). Both endpoints are in data/revealed_calibration.json under reference_densities. The rule then maximises P(DTI > floor) — the floor being the group's best reported score, carried in data/revealed_budget.json — and breaks ties toward the larger novel fraction, because the brief requires a unique artefact and a mean cannot see that. 7 The two views, and the conditional-independence test

The brief requires the Blum & Mitchell premise to be tested rather than assumed: correlate the two views' out-of-fold errors on labelled negatives over spatial blocks, and abandon the arm if they are strongly correlated. It also requires a single-view baseline, so that co-training cannot quietly amplify one view's bias. Both are below, including the reading that co-training does not win here.
