Validation: three instruments, and what each one can and cannot see

Every claim on this page is a number from evidence/, produced on folds built from the official label file, with the visible catalogue masked out of both the allowed emission set and the score — the treatment the organiser applies (thread 11516, posts #2 and #4, chrisk-dd, Sep 21).

The third column is measured on the real label file (knowledge/05_instruments_and_what_each_can_see.md), not assumed. It is why this repo runs two instruments. The shipped configuration is not the one that maximises the flattering number, and not the minimum of the two either — the two regimes are disjoint populations, so the choice is made on their sum by scripts/composite_split.py, whose control and tie-break rules are printed under its own table below and stored in evidence/composite.json.

4 Two-regime composite: the sweep that picked the shipped file

Corridor mass (1–6 px off a visible trace, where 88.5 % of the truncation instrument's truth lives) is ranked by the ranking that wins there; far-field mass (never-mapped structures, the only population the whole-component instrument can see) by the ranking that wins there; and the share between them at a fixed total budget is swept. Each candidate is compared against random emission in the same region, at the same split and the same budget — anything else compares a ranking against the best thing in the sweep rather than against chance.

loading data/composite.json…

Two honest caveats. (i) The rule's control was wrong in the first version of the script — it took max over every arm containing “random”, which handed the corridor arm's own score to the bar it was supposed to clear, and made the shipped arm look like it lost to chance. The fix and the recomputation are in scripts/composite_recount.py, and both selections are in the JSON. (ii) The selected configuration clears the control bar on both instruments and misses the pre-registered “+0.010 over naive union” bar on the truncation instrument, so it is not promoted: it is the best of what we measured, which is a weaker statement, and the build records say so (promoted: false, forced: true).

The promotion rule, applied literally. registry/preregistration.json requires the shipped arm to beat the naive union by 0.010 DTI on the folds and to beat uniform-random emission at equal budget on ≥ 3 of 4 folds. The co-training arm failed on both instruments — beats_union: not OK, placement_not_mass: not OK, fold_support: 0/4 — so it is not what we ship, and no weekly slot was spent on it. These tables are that failure's record, kept published.

6 Layer screen, for reference

The per-layer precision that motivated the split, reproduced from evidence/layer_screen.json: the best single surface layer (B_scarp_p900, precision@40k 0.493, 5.73× base rate) is the strongest localiser on isolated catalogue pixels, and the best geophysical one (A_strain_inv_reg, 0.310) is the strongest on the continuation population. The inverse relationship of A_depth_base_rank (0.032) is not a failure — it is the statement that the catalogue lives where the cover is thin.
