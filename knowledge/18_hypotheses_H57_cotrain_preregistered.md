# H57 — corrected geophysical/surface views and fault candidates

**Status: preregistered before feature construction, fit, or holdout.** The protocol is frozen in `registry/h57_preregistration.json`. The primary label is geological fault mapping. Geothermal-fluid and resource context is motivation only, not the prediction target.

## Evidence boundary

The restored competition rasters are SHA-256 pinned owner-maintained mirrors, not bytes authenticated by DrivenData. Band 6 is a special provenance risk: its GeoTIFF tag says magnetic tilt/total curvature, but a seeded comparison against an owner-mirrored GeoDAWN total-count raster gives near-perfect rank agreement. The official USGS GeoDAWN release establishes that radiometric products exist, but the official page does not map the competition's band 6. H57 therefore assigns band 6 to View B as a measured working hypothesis and keeps the conflict visible; it does not call that mapping organizer-confirmed.

The 0-valued/catalogue-zero pool is not a verified absence class. All negative-error and holdout statements below are diagnostics against an incomplete mapped-fault catalogue. A weak error correlation is not proof of conditional independence.

## Primary test and decision rule

H57-H1 compares View A, View B, their fixed max-union control, and an asymmetric disagreement-aware field on the same four whole-component spatial folds, 80-pixel buffer, learner settings, sample rows, and metric-aware emission budget. It uses only the mirrored core rasters. If the blockwise OOF negative-proxy errors are strongly correlated, undefined, or too sparse, pseudo-label exchange is abandoned and the predeclared View-B-only fallback is used. Pseudo-labels, if permitted, are whole connected components contained in a 50×50 block and in the training domain; components crossing a fold or the 3-pixel catalogue collar are rejected whole. If the cap binds, components are ordered by descending mean teacher score, then first row-major pixel; whole components are added while they fit, and an oversized remainder is skipped rather than trimmed. The same view random state is used on the same sampled rows.

A candidate is not eligible for a weekly submission slot unless it improves mean DTI by at least 0.005 over the strongest matched arm, wins at least three of four paired folds, clears the historical internal holdout hurdle in the registration, is non-identical and materially distinct from every accessible aligned prior and the matched max-union, passes an on-disk format/read-back gate, and then receives independent confirmation. No portal upload is authorized by this registration. No result here can establish an organizer score or a public-leaderboard improvement.
