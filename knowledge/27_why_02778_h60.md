## 1. The metric, reduced

From the official page (<https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/>),
α = 0.2, β = 0.8, R = 300 m. With `FNw = |G| − TPw` (an identity, pinned in
`tests/test_metric.py`):

```
DTI = T / ( 0.2·T + 0.2·(S − M) + 0.8·|G| )
T = Σ_g max_x p(x)k(d)   credited mass
S = Σ_x p(x)             emitted mass
M = Σ_x p(x)·max_g k(d)  emitted mass that sits within 300 m of some truth pixel
```

Two consequences decide everything:

* **Every emitted pixel more than 300 m from every truth pixel is a pure loss of 0.2.**
  The marginal rule `c·(1 − 0.2·DTI) > 0.2·DTI·(1 − m_x)` gives a bar of 0.0556 at
  DTI = 0.2778, and a pixel 300 m from all truth has `c = 0` and `m_x = 0`, so it always fails.
* **Binary `{0,1}` is optimal.** For a single pixel of weight λ on a truth pixel,
  `DTI = λk/(0.2λ + 0.8)` increases in λ, so any accepted pixel should carry 1.0.

The local pixel fact is exact: the H33-labelled 37,654-cell bitmap is a strict subset of the
separate 44,090-cell bitmap; their difference is 6,436 cells, all 100–200 m from the local
known-fault mask. The 0.2778 and 0.2600 values are owner-reported associations, not organizer
receipts linking scores to these file hashes.

The earlier inversion used the reported values in a simplified DTI equation and additionally

credit. Under those assumptions it returned `|G| = 14,088.7` and a 5,223-credit scenario. Neither

Official staff confirms the known-fault mask is pixel-exact and only new-fault truth is scored; staff

mask does not imply zero credit or automatic penalty. No cause of a reported score change is

provenance classification.

## 4. A tomography attempt that failed, recorded so nobody repeats it

With 13 emissions and 13 owner-reported score values, the historical analysis linearised the metric in a relaxed truth density `g`; this is a conditional identifiability exercise, not truth recovery:

```
Σ_x g(x)·[ W_i(x)(1 + 0.6 s_i) + 0.2 s_i K_i(x) ] = s_i(0.2 S_i + 0.8 N)
W_i(x) = max_{y∈P_i} k(d(x,y)),   K_i(x) = Σ_{y∈P_i} k(d(x,y))
```

Assuming `g` uniform over the footprint, the thirteen priors imply `N` of **11,349 … 499,618**
(and several are negative, i.e. no `N` at all) — see `work/f3_tomography.py` output. Allowing
`g` to vary over 12,051 blocks with box bounds, the equality system is **infeasible at every
`N` from 1,000 to 60,000**. Thirteen scalars cannot localise a 14,000–27,000-pixel set, and
the first-order `E[max] ≈ Σ` approximation for `M` breaks exactly where `g` is concentrated,
which is where faults are. **Killed: recovering the hidden truth from public scores.**

## 7. What remains unmeasured

No defensible score projection follows from these data. The earlier `5,223 / 37,654` density and the `|G|`-based break-even calculations are conditional outputs of owner-reported values and assumptions, not measured hidden-truth credit. The hide-and-recover assay is a separate HOLDOUT-DTI measurement on withheld catalogue segments; it is not an organizer score and does not establish off-catalogue performance.

What is actually available, in order of certainty:

1. **Do not infer credit from distance to the known-fault mask alone.** Apply the pixel-exact known-fault mask as stated by staff; new-fault truth may lie within 300 m.
2. **Keep evidence classes separate.** HOLDOUT-DTI is a local hide-and-recover measure, public-board values are team-level observations, owner reports are not receipts, and no organizer-confirmed score-to-file mapping is present.
3. **Test geology on preregistered, spatially blocked holdout.** Any future candidate must use the shared protocol and beat the comparable baseline before separate slot consideration.
