# H97 — tilt-depth A-only disagreement on the same spatial holdout as H82/H84

**Pre-registration, frozen before any H97 supervised fit, canary, holdout score, or placement.** The only executed candidate will be H97-A below. The H82/H84 evaluation mask is fixed in advance at 53,186 withheld catalogue-positive pixels; H96's 60,894-positive mask is explicitly not used for cross-round score comparison. No submission slot is requested or spent.

## Scope and source discipline

H97 remains in the single geophysical/surface co-training-disagreement lane. Its purpose is to test whether a magnetic source-depth cue can make the A-only (geophysical-confidence / surface-abstention) stratum more useful when paired with the strongest same-mask H84 surface/variogram comparator. It is not a leaderboard forecast. The GEMSDOE32 owner project page describes H33-2-B2 as a downloadable artifact, reports 37,654 dots and a projected 0.2747, and explicitly says there is no organizer score receipt. The public leaderboard snapshot separately shows `extradr19` at 0.2778 (#22); the page does not link that leaderboard row to the H33-2-B2 file, so the attribution and score provenance remain unresolved. Neither 0.2778 nor the owner projection is organizer-confirmed for H33-2-B2. The #1 0.3774 and #8 0.3195 rows are also contextual board snapshots, not H97 or H33 receipts; no HOLDOUT-DTI value will be translated into any board score.

The official USGS GeoDAWN record (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ) is the named, free source for the radiometric and magnetic-derived context layers already present in the project cache. The local external rasters are owner-mirrored, integrity-pinned bytes, not a fresh authenticated download from USGS and not organizer-authenticated competition inputs. No additional external layer is needed for H97. Novelty/lane checks are pinned to the frozen 526-blob owner-repository census `evidence/ctd5_prior_inventory.json` (524 eligible aligned rasters from 50 repositories; inventory SHA-256 `d8b4b7e15482636cefd52efa1085745ae2cead6562f095532beba7a12c98a7c1`, 466,963 bytes); all eligible rasters must be restored and verified before a lane verdict.

## H97-A frozen implementation and holdout protocol

1. **Eligibility/mask:** rebuild/verify `gems52.structural.FeatureStore` with the shared external extension; `valid` must contain 4,593,171 cells and the four shared `spatial.folds(..., buffer_px=80)` truth counts must sum to **53,186**. Any mismatch stops the fit rather than comparing against H82/H84 across masks. Fold regions hide whole 8-connected catalogue components plus the 80-pixel spatial buffer; catalogue-based features, if any, are derived only from visible faults. The shared evaluator masks visible catalogue pixels exactly and scores pooled DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8 and the 300 m triangular kernel.
2. **Views:** A = `view_A_with_external` plus two label-free magnetic tilt-depth raster channels from cached band 3/9. B = `view_B_with_external` plus H84 DVA2/HVA/COH channels for bands 12/19 only. No channel appears in both views. A separate H84 `B_DVA2` model is refit with the exact H84 arm/channel definition, seed, row draw, fold mask, budget and evaluator as the historical control; it is explicitly identified as mixed-view by content.
3. **Tilt-depth proxy:** `θ = atan2(band9, abs(band3)+ε)`, `ε = 0.01 × median(abs(band3))` over the eligible cache. The registered proxy is half the sum of Euclidean distances to the nearest `θ=+45°` and `θ=−45°` contour, multiplied by 100 m and capped at 5,000 m; it is zero outside the `|θ|≤45°` contour band. A second channel is `exp(-abs(θ)/(π/4))`. This is a rasterised nearest-contour approximation to Salem et al.'s contour-spacing rule, not a connected-contour solution or ground-truth depth. All thresholds, clipping, and scales are frozen before fitting.
4. **Leakage canary:** direction-insensitive single-feature AUC on each held-out fold for every cached learner feature plus both tilt-depth channels and every H84 channel used by H97. Any feature AUC >0.90 is a leakage alarm and stops fitting until disproven; it is not quietly dropped.
5. **Single-view and independence tests:** fit A and B separately on each fold's visible positives and catalogue-zero training negatives. Test View-A out-of-quadrant sufficiency against the standing 0.60 mean / 0.55 min-fold screen. Correlate spatial-block OOF negative-error MSE/FPR using `gems52.spatial.negative_block_errors` and `independence`, 50-pixel blocks, at least 20 blocks, 4-pixel negative collar, and |correlation| <0.60. Catalogue-zero pixels are proxies, not verified fault absence.
6. **Conditional pseudo-label training:** `knowledge/03` N-1 refutes plain confident-to-abstaining exchange on two prior instruments, and H91/H96 document repeated View-A sufficiency failures. H97 permits at most one bidirectional exchange only if (a) the pre-exchange canary is clean, (b) the registered spatial-block negative-error independence test is measured and below |ρ|=0.60, and (c) View A clears mean OOF AUC ≥0.60 and every-fold AUC ≥0.55. Otherwise exchange is abandoned and pseudo-label count is zero. If permitted, the shared `gems52.spatial.whole_pseudo_segments` helper is mandatory: q95 donor-confidence, receiver q35–q65 abstention, 50×50 blocks, 5-pixel component minimum, 2,000-pixel cap, visible-catalogue exclusion, and the fold's 80-pixel holdout buffer. As in the shared cotrain routine, both transfer directions must yield at least 200 accepted pseudo pixels or the entire exchange is skipped. Post-exchange fits use the fixed seed `61,052 + 1,000 + fold`. Held-out labels are never used to select or train pseudo labels. Passing weak negative-error correlation alone is not proof of conditional independence.
7. **Candidate field and comparison:** on each fold's allowed region, compute percentile ranks `rA`,`rB` without using held truth. `A_only = (rA≥0.95) & (0.35≤rB≤0.65)`. The primary score is `0.80*rB + 0.20*((rA−0.95)/0.05)` on A-only pixels, and `0.80*rB` elsewhere. Compare at 9,400 dots/fold and 3-pixel minimum spacing against strict single-B, single-A, `max(A,B)` union, random, and the refit H84 `B_DVA2` incumbent. Report pooled HOLDOUT-DTI and paired 95% physical-block bootstrap intervals (1,000 draws, 200-pixel blocks, seed 61,052). The result must beat H84 `B_DVA2` with paired lower CI >0 to clear the registered holdout gate; no slot is implied.
8. **Surface before placement and final lane gate:** before any dots, compare the finite [0,1] cross-fitted H97 field against every available aligned registry raster with `gems52.gates.lane_report(phase="surface")`. Stop if any Spearman rank correlation exceeds 0.90. Only if it passes, place 37,654 dots from the same fixed field on the full visible catalogue-free pool, 200 m catalogue collar, 3-pixel minimum spacing, with the shared `nodes.spacing_select`. Then run the full registry dots lane/uniqueness checks; >70% of dots within 3 pixels of any single prior is DUPLICATE/STOP. Also check the candidate is not the union of the two views and write a reasoning row for every emitted A-only candidate.
9. **GeoTIFF:** shared `gems52.submission_writer`; single-band float32, EPSG:32611, exact sample shape/transform/bounds, finite [0,1], no NaN, zeros outside the eligible footprint. Research output only unless every preregistered gate passes; no upload, no promotion, and no slot selection in this round.
