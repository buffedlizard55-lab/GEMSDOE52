Why this submission

Co-training between a geophysical view (A) and a surface view (B) with disagreement as the discovery signal (Blum & Mitchell, COLT 1998, doi:10.1145/279943.279962). Two HistGradientBoosting classifiers are trained on disjoint feature subsets: View A has 17 potential-field, strain, and seismicity bands; View B has 2 DEM-derived curvature/slope bands plus GeoDAWN radiometric and 3DEP LiDAR scarp features. The conditional-independence assumption is verified empirically on the labelled-negative out-of-fold residuals (Pearson r = +0.190, well below the 0.60 abandonment gate).

The discovery signal is disagreement. Cells where View A is confident and View B abstains become A-only candidates (subsurface signature with no topographic scarp = fault may be buried beneath cover). Cells where View B is confident and View A abstains are kept as the “surface artifact suspicion” branch (suspect roads or erosion lines).

Every A-only candidate cell carries a geological reasoning record: the top-3 View-A bands by robust z-score, so a Phase 2 reviewer can audit each predicted fault.
