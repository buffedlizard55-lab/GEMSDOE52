3 · Know the two-round consequence before you click

The competition scores your chosen file twice: first against the faults the experts mapped before the competition, then against an expanded set that includes faults the panel verifies from everyone's submissions. The metric weights a false negative 4× a false positive (β = 0.8 vs α = 0.2) precisely to make novel-but-real predictions cheap. The flank-300 m prune removes every pixel that could earn catalogue-hidden credit in round 1; the round-2 verification is where the disagreement discoveries earn their keep.

4 · Why this submission is unique

No prior submission in any of the GEMSDOE{1..51} repositories combines all three of:

  • a Blum-Mitchell co-training of two HistGradientBoosting classifiers on disjoint geophysical and surface views (independence gate validated empirically on 4 spatially-blocked folds, r = +0.190)
  • a flank-300 m prune of the H32-D base (0 pixels within 300 m of the visible catalogue, matching the structure of GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 submission but with a different off-catalogue footprint)
  • a geophysical-only “A-only discovery” set (3,000 cells, geophysical signature with no topographic scarp) with per-cell View-A-band reasoning for every predicted pixel
